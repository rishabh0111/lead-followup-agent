# Lead follow-up

A small packaging-equipment seller's sales team: which leads each rep should contact today, and what to say to them.

## Language

### People and leads

**Lead**:
A buyer at a company that one rep is working to sell to.
_Avoid_: Prospect, contact, customer, account

**Rep**:
One of the five sales reps; each lead belongs to exactly one.
_Avoid_: Salesperson, agent, user

**Buyer**:
The person on the lead's side who talks to the rep.
_Avoid_: Client, customer

**Source**:
Where the lead came from, such as IndiaMART, a trade show or a referral.
_Avoid_: Channel, origin

### Conversation

**Chat**:
All messages between a lead's rep and buyer, in time order.
_Avoid_: Conversation, thread, history

**Message**:
One line in a chat, sent by either the rep or the buyer.

**Draft**:
The suggested next message from the rep to the buyer, which the rep may edit and mark as sent.
_Avoid_: Follow-up, template, suggestion

**Last contact date**:
The date of the most recent message between rep and buyer.

### Assessment and scheduling

**Tag**:
How likely a lead is to buy soon: Hot, Warm or Cold.
_Avoid_: Status, score, temperature

**Reason**:
One line explaining why a lead has its tag.

**Requested date**:
A date the buyer asked to be contacted on.

**Follow-up date**:
The date the rep should next contact the lead.
_Avoid_: "Follow-up" on its own, which is ambiguous between this date and the draft

**Overdue**:
A lead whose follow-up date is before today (India time).

**Due today**:
A lead whose follow-up date is today (India time).

**Today list**:
The overdue and due-today leads, most urgent first.
_Avoid_: Follow-up list, inbox
